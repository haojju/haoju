===== Taiwan government open data: public transit station directory =====
The station names and coordinates in transit-stations.json are derived from
Taipei Metro, Taichung Metro, Taiwan Railways, Kaohsiung Metro, and Kaohsiung
Light Rail datasets published on data.gov.tw. These datasets are provided under
the Government Open Data License, version 1.0. Individual source links are
included in the data file and in the station-search attribution panel.
